📘 LAB Color Space Explanation

L → Lightness

Represents the brightness of the fabric color. Lower values indicate darker shades, while higher values indicate lighter shades.

A → Green ↔ Red

Represents the balance between green and red tones inside the denim shade.

B → Blue ↔ Yellow

Represents the balance between blue and yellow tones inside the fabric.
